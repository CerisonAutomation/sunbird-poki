# Visual / In-Browser QA — every screen, real gameplay

**Date:** 2026-09-30
**Build:** `arena/01a0f277-sunbird-poki` @ `177fa85`, Vite dev server on `0.0.0.0:5173` (the same server surfaced to you as the live preview).
**Method:** headless Chromium 153 (`@sparticuz/chromium` + `puppeteer-core`, SwiftShader/ANGLE software GL), driven by CDP. Navigation by `[data-action]`. Screenshots in `shots/`.
**Coverage:** 18 menu destinations, 5 viewports (640×360, 836×470, 1031×580, 390×844, 844×390) plus 1280×720, launch + sustained flight + pause + continue-offer, DOM overflow/tap-target/scroll probes, console + `pageerror` capture per screen.

> **Honest caveats up front.** (1) All rendering here is *software* GL. The frame numbers below are a rasteriser artefact and are **not** a performance verdict — ignore them except as a relative signal. (2) The sandbox blocks the Poki SDK CDN, so every portal-gated surface (rewarded video, midroll, `commercialBreak`) fell back to its non-portal branch and could **not** be inspected live. (3) The headless font set is minimal, which is why glyph fallback shows up so starkly; on a well-provisioned desktop it will be less bad — but the underlying decision is still wrong (see V-3).

---

## Verdict

The game is in far better shape than a screenshot-free audit would suggest. Zero uncaught `pageerror`s across every screen and every viewport. Zero console errors other than the two expected blocked-CDN entries. Zero tap targets under 44×44 px in the flight HUD on any mobile viewport — that is genuinely rare and worth saying out loud. ESC pauses **and** resumes, which is a hard Poki requirement that most submissions fail.

What's wrong is almost entirely **framing and readability**, not correctness. Three things would get flagged in a Poki review, and one is a real playability bug.

## Critical

### V-1 — The scene becomes unreadable at dusk (playability, not cosmetics)
`shots/g-gameover.png`, taken at 1.65 km into a continuous run.

By late run the palette collapses to maroon sky over teal/navy/near-black terrain. The player bird is a small red-orange shape against dark teal hills; the horizon line, the water plane and the hill silhouettes all sit within a few percent luminance of each other. Compare `shots/p-1280x720-flight2.png` (389 m, bright, perfectly legible) with the same HUD at 1.65 km — the HUD is still fine, the *world* isn't.

This is the single most damaging finding, because it gets worse exactly as the player gets further, i.e. it punishes the players who are doing well. A one-button game where you cannot see the terrain you are about to hit is not a difficulty curve, it's a fairness failure.

**Fix direction:** floor the terrain/sky luminance separation. Either clamp the dusk grade so foreground terrain never drops below a fixed contrast ratio against sky, or add a rim/fresnel light on hill crests and a guaranteed-contrast silhouette pass on the player bird that survives any time-of-day grade. Do not solve it by shortening the day cycle — the dusk look is the game's best art.

### V-2 — Every panel is a fixed-width column that gets clipped, at every size
`shots/v-640x360-menu.png`, `shots/s-mode-select.png`, `shots/s-open-settings.png`.

The home panel is 1,937–2,187 px of content in a 310–762 px window. Measured:

| Viewport | Panel content height | Visible | Screens of scrolling |
|---|---|---|---|
| 640×360 (Poki floor) | 1,937 px | 310 px | **6.2** |
| 1280×720 | 1,999 px | 686 px | 2.9 |
| 390×844 | 2,187 px | 762 px | 2.9 |
| Game modes @ any size | 1,817 px | 310–638 px | 2.8–5.9 |

It *does* scroll (`overflow-y: auto`, wheel confirmed working, inner `.paper-card` is the scroller) so nothing is unreachable — but there is **no scroll affordance**. The card is simply sheared off mid-row at the viewport bottom in every single capture. At 1280×720 the Game modes list shows 6 of 17 entries and **all nine PvP circuits, AI PvP and split-screen are below the fold with nothing indicating they exist.** A first-time player at 640×360 sees Fly now, PvP, AI PvP, Shop, Settings, and a cut-off edge.

Secondary: `.overlay.menu` itself measures `scrollHeight 768` vs `clientHeight 720` with `overflow: hidden` at 1280×720, and 48 px of overflow at 1031×580 — that outer 48 px is clipped and *not* scrollable. That one is a genuine bug, not just a design choice.

**Fix direction:** (a) fix the outer `.overlay` 48 px overflow. (b) Give the scroller a persistent affordance — a bottom fade mask plus a styled always-visible scrollbar track. (c) On landscape viewports ≥ 900 px wide, the panel should go two-column instead of staying a 440 px strip with ~50 % dead background on both sides. (d) Deliberately shorten the home surface — see V-4.

### V-3 — Icons are bare Unicode glyphs and fall back to tofu
`shots/v-mobile-portrait-menu.png` (three ▯), `shots/p-1280x720-flight2.png` (the **pause button** renders as ▯▯), `shots/g-gameover.png`, `shots/s-open-live.png`, `shots/s-mode-select.png`.

Sources: `HUD.ts:379` uses `❙❙` (U+2759) for pause, `MenuIcons.ts:140` maps `pause`/`castle`/`hourglass`/`spin` to `❙❙ ⛫︎ ⧖ ◷`, `HUD.ts:2153` and `HUD.ts:2254` use `✕` (U+2715), and `✦` (U+2726) is used throughout as the premium mark.

U+2759 and U+2715 are not in the base font set of lean Android WebViews, many Linux distros, or stripped Chromebook images. The **pause control** — the one button Poki explicitly cares about — rendering as two empty boxes is not an acceptable failure mode, and it is exactly what happened here.

**Fix direction:** replace every functional glyph (pause, close, back) with the inline SVG the codebase already uses elsewhere (`arrowRightSvg()`, `menuIcon()`). Decorative glyphs like `✦` can stay if a fallback font is declared, but functional controls must not depend on font coverage.

## Significant

### V-4 — The pre-gameplay surface contradicts Poki's core UX rule
Poki: *"minimise the number of UI screens, ideally drop players straight into gameplay."*

Home, in order, before a new player has flown once: title card, **Fly now**, PvP, AI PvP, Shop, Settings, Daily Challenge banner, Perfect Circuit banner, a 5-item START HERE checklist, then Endless, Same-screen 1v1, Squad, Top pilots, Your progress, Story, Trophies, Island Atlas, Your scores, Account, Nest Pass. That is 20 destinations and ~3–6 screens of scroll. There are **54 buttons in the DOM** on the home screen.

`Fly now` is correctly the first, largest, highest-contrast element — credit where due. But everything under it is a reason not to press it. A brand-new player's first frame should be roughly: title, Fly now, and a mute toggle. Everything else earns its way in after run one.

**Fix direction:** collapse Shop/Progress/Trophies/Atlas/Scores/Account/Pass/Squad/Board behind a single "More" destination or a bottom tab strip. Gate the START HERE checklist and both banners behind "has completed ≥ 1 run". Target ≤ 6 home destinations, zero scroll at 640×360.

### V-5 — The pause card is a second main menu
`shots/p-pause.png`. "Keep flying" is correctly the biggest, warmest, most obvious control. Under it: Sound, Settings, Shop, Global Board, My Scores, Nest Pass, Squad, Trophies, Progress, Fullscreen, Restart flight, Exit to menu. **Twelve controls on a pause screen.**

Poki's `commercialBreak()` rule is that you call it when the player exits a pause *back into gameplay*. Every one of those ten side-doors is a path out of pause that isn't back into gameplay, which multiplies the surface where that rule can be got wrong — and it invites the player to wander off mid-run.

**Fix direction:** Keep flying / Restart / Exit / Sound / Settings / Fullscreen. Drop the six meta destinations; they belong in the menu, not in the middle of a run.

### V-6 — Five notification channels compete during flight
At 1.65 km simultaneously on screen: a centre-screen objective toast ("Climb to 40 m altitude ▯ +40"), a green "Goal complete +40" pill top-right, a "Marathon 1500/1500" progress bar bottom-left, and **two** stacked objective cards bottom-centre ("Grab 1 sky gems", "Hat Trick"). The bottom-centre pair sits directly over the airspace the bird descends into.

Measured HUD density during normal flight: 88 visible nodes, **17 simultaneous live text readouts**. For a one-button game.

**Fix direction:** one notification queue, one at a time, one location. Move the persistent objective cards out of the lower-centre flight corridor (lower-left, above the altitude ribbon). Cap concurrent objectives at one.

### V-7 — Illustration assets don't scale down
`shots/v-640x360-flight.png` — the AI PvP screen at 640×360 is almost entirely one enormous bird illustration overflowing its card, with the card's actual content pushed off-screen. The art is presumably sized for a desktop card and never constrained for the Poki floor resolution.

**Fix direction:** cap hero art with `max-height: min(30vh, 160px)` and hide it entirely below ~400 px of panel height.

## Minor

- **`best 0 m` while the run is at 1.65 km.** The personal-best readout under the distance counter stays at `0 m` for a fresh save throughout a record run. Either hide it until a best exists, or live-update it — showing "best 0 m" next to "1.65 km" reads as a bug to the player.
- **The sun sprite overdraws the daylight bar.** At 1280×720 the sun disc renders over the centre-top daylight meter and its "DAYLIGHT" label (`p-1280x720-flight2.png`, `g-gameover.png`). Either move the meter or z-order the HUD above the sun bloom.
- **Green "Goal complete" pill.** Not a rewarded button, so not a violation — but Poki reviewers pattern-match on green reward-shaped pills in the top-right. Recolour to the game's gold.
- **Menu background is dead space.** At 1280×720 a 440 px panel is centred over ~840 px of unused attract-flight backdrop. The attract bird reads as an orange blob at that scale (`s-open-settings.png`, `00-boot.png`) — either push it much closer to camera so it's a hero shot, or blur/darken the backdrop so it's honestly a backdrop.
- **Space does not resume from pause.** ESC and P both toggle correctly (verified: `flying → pause-actions → flying → pause-actions`). Space from pause does nothing. Given Space is the dive key this is defensible, but Poki's wording mentions spacebar; consider accepting Space-to-resume with a short input lockout so the resume press doesn't also dive.

## What passed cleanly

- **Zero uncaught exceptions and zero non-CDN console errors** across 18 screens × 6 viewports and sustained gameplay.
- **Zero sub-44 px tap targets** in the flight HUD at 640×360, 390×844 and 844×390.
- **ESC pause/resume works**, including the sub-screen-collapses-first behaviour — a hard Poki requirement, met.
- **No page-level scroll** — `document.body.scrollHeight === innerHeight` at every viewport. Poki's "must not scroll the parent page" requirement holds.
- **First input, not load, starts gameplay** — the home screen sits idle until `Fly now`.
- Settings, Race Lobby and the pause card are well-composed, legible, and clearly written. The copy throughout ("Your run is safe", "Resume keeps your momentum") is better than most of this genre.

## Not verifiable in this environment

Rewarded video and midroll behaviour, `commercialBreak()` on pause-exit, the 🎬 clapperboard label, the ad-blocker-detected no-reward path, and the standard-continue-vs-rewarded button sizing rule. All are portal-gated and the SDK CDN is blocked here. The continue offer that appeared after the long run was the non-portal branch: **"Second wind?" → "Spend 80 (you have 100)" (286×50) vs "Let it sleep" (92×50)**. On a portal build the primary must become a rewarded video with a clapperboard and a non-green treatment, with a free/standard continue at least as large beside or above it. **This needs a human playtest on a real Poki test build.**

Frame pacing likewise: SwiftShader reported a 4.5–8.8 fps median. That is the software rasteriser, not the game. Real-GPU profiling on a mid-range Android device is still outstanding.

## Suggested order of work

1. V-1 dusk contrast floor — playability.
2. V-3 SVG icons for pause/close/back — a broken pause button is a review blocker.
3. V-2 outer overlay 48 px overflow + scroll affordance + two-column ≥ 900 px.
4. V-4 / V-5 cut the home surface to ≤ 6 destinations and the pause card to 6 controls.
5. V-6 single notification queue, clear the lower-centre flight corridor.
6. V-7 hero-art clamp, then the minors.
