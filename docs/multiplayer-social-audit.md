# Multiplayer and social audit

Sunbird has a strong comparison loop: live PvP rooms, AI fallback, deterministic pace ghosts, leaderboards, squads, clubs, quick emotes, and race recap. The redesigned menu makes PvP and AI PvP first-class choices while preserving a low-friction solo daily course.

The remaining depth risk is social persistence: a player can compare and race, but the strongest group habit still depends on returning to the same squad or room. The next safe product increment is a lightweight recurring squad challenge with fixed emotes and server-side rate limits; free-text chat must remain disabled in portal builds.

Current safeguards include curated portal names, escaped roster content, no unmoderated chat in portal editions, AI fallback when a room is empty, and deterministic ghosts so a player always has a credible rival.
