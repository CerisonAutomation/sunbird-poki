Sunbird — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026 · Applies to the game Sunbird in every edition (web, itch.io, CrazyGames and Poki).

Short version: Sunbird does not ask for your name, email, phone number, address or any other personal information. It collects no analytics of its own on portal builds. Progress, settings and high scores are stored on your device, and — on Poki, when you are signed in — synced to your Poki cloud save by the Poki SDK. There is no advertising network of ours in the build; ads on portals are delivered by the portal itself.

1. Information we do not collect

  • No account, email, password or real name is requested by the game.
  • No contact lists, precise location, camera, microphone or files.
  • No third-party analytics, trackers, fingerprinting or advertising SDKs are bundled.
  • No in-game chat or free-text entry that other players can read: pilot names are chosen from a curated list.

3. Poki platform features

On Poki, the game integrates the official Poki SDK. These platform features can process data under Poki's own privacy policy, which is the controller for them:

  • Cloud gamesaves. When you are signed in to Poki, the Poki SDK syncs your game save (localStorage) to your Poki account so progress follows you between devices. Keys the game marks local-only — caches, recordings, diagnostics — are excluded with the documented poki_ignore prefix. Saves are limited to 1 MB gzip; if a save exceeded that, cloud sync would simply be disabled for that player.
  • User Accounts. The game reads getUser() after loading to greet you by your Poki username. Signing in is only ever triggered by you pressing Sign in in Settings. We do not receive or store your Poki credentials; a session token, if requested, is single-use and expires in about a minute.
  • AUDS (leaderboards, ghost runs, share codes). Scores and shared runs are stored by Poki's AUDS service for the game id configured in the build, and are readable by anyone who has the entry's id or code.
  • Netlib (multiplayer). Live races connect players directly over WebRTC data channels (peer-to-peer), with Poki-hosted signalling and TURN relays used only to establish or relay the connection. In-race traffic is your pilot position, skin and emote — no personal data.
  • Ads. Commercial and rewarded breaks are delivered by Poki. We receive aggregate reporting from Poki's dashboard; the game itself sets no advertising identifiers.

4. Other editions

The standalone web build, the itch.io build and the CrazyGames build ship without AUDS, without Poki features and without any tracker. If you run a self-hosted Sunbird server, leaderboard entries are stored on that server; the operator of that server is the controller for them.

5. Children

Sunbird is made for a general audience including children. The game contains no chat, no player-authored text, no purchases and no data collection aimed at children. Pilot names are drawn from a curated list of fictional call signs.

6. Your choices

  • Clearing your browser's site data removes the local save and local caches.
  • Not signing in to Poki keeps the game fully playable; cloud sync simply stays off.
  • On Poki, requests about platform-side data (account, cloud saves, ad data) are handled by Poki under its own privacy policy.

7. Changes and contact

Material changes to this policy will be reflected here with an updated date. Questions about this policy or about data in the game: privacy@cerison.dev.
